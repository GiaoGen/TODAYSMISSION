# TODAYSMISSION Character Lock
## Character 05 — Otter

Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0
Role: Production Character

---

## 1. Identity

Character 05 is a soft, blankly adorable otter belonging to the TODAYSMISSION character universe.

Its identity is defined by:

- cocoa-brown rounded body
- large warm-cream belly / lower-face region
- very small rounded ears
- compact short proportions
- large white eyes with simple movable pupils
- gentle blank / slightly slow-to-react gaze
- tiny simple nose and mouth
- short thick limbs
- thick soft tapered tail
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation rendering

The character must remain immediately recognizable across poses, expressions, scenes, clothing, accessories and camera angles.

---

## 2. Character Role

The otter is the adorably blank, relaxed and slightly slow-to-react member of the family.

Core personality:

- blankly cute
- gentle
- laid-back
- innocent
- soft
- calm
- slightly slow to react

The otter should feel endearingly spaced-out, not clever, sly or hyperactive.

---

## 3. Core Silhouette

The otter has a compact, rounded, low-center-of-gravity body.

Required:

- short overall stature
- wide rounded torso
- large belly mass
- small head/body separation
- short thick limbs
- thick integrated tail
- low center of gravity

Avoid:

- tall slim proportions
- long torso
- long limbs
- realistic otter anatomy
- long neck
- narrow waist

The silhouette should remain soft and stable.

---

## 4. Face

The face should remain extremely simple.

Required:

- broad warm-cream lower-face area
- small simple nose
- minimal mouth
- very small rounded ears
- large readable eyes
- uncluttered face

Avoid:

- strongly projected muzzle
- detailed whisker pads
- realistic snout anatomy
- detailed nostrils
- complex fur markings

Whiskers may appear subtly but must remain secondary.

---

## 5. Eyes

The eyes are a key expression feature.

Required:

- clear white eye areas
- simple dark pupils
- readable gaze direction
- soft rounded geometry
- gentle, slightly blank default look

Allowed during acting:

- widening
- compression
- closing
- directional gaze
- slight asymmetry
- temporary size changes

Avoid:

- tiny black-dot eyes
- glossy kawaii eyes
- anime irises
- eyelashes
- realistic eyes

The gaze should preserve the otter's mild, slightly slow-to-react personality.

---

## 6. Ears

The ears are:

- very small
- round
- placed low on the head
- visually secondary
- integrated into the silhouette

Avoid:

- large ears
- pointed ears
- detailed inner-ear anatomy

---

## 7. Belly / Cream Region

The warm-cream region is a major visual anchor.

It should:

- cover the belly prominently
- connect naturally to the lower-face area
- remain soft and rounded
- stay visually simple

It may deform with perspective, sitting, lying down, squash/stretch or swimming.

Do not redesign its basic placement.

---

## 8. Tail

The tail is:

- thick
- soft
- rounded
- tapered
- naturally integrated into the body
- slightly upward-curved in neutral poses

It may:

- trail behind
- flatten
- bend
- move during swimming
- compress while sitting or lying down

It must never become:

- long and whip-like
- thin
- sharply pointed
- realistic
- detached from the body

---

## 9. Limbs

Arms and legs are:

- short
- thick
- rounded
- simple

Hands and feet remain simplified.

Limb geometry may stretch or compress during acting but must return to the canonical short, soft structure.

## 11. Rendering Language

Use the TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style.

Required:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- subtle line-weight variation
- matte color blocks
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-like tonal variation
- finish quality matching the Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- raw sketch rendering
- heavy fur texture
- overly fuzzy surface treatment
- glossy AI mascot rendering
- perfect vector geometry
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- smooth synthetic gradients
- photorealism

Texture must remain secondary to the clean color shapes.

---

## 14. Acting Rules

The otter should act like a soft, slightly blank animation performer.

Useful acting tendencies include:

- delayed-looking gaze
- slight pause or hesitation
- relaxed posture
- gentle inward hands
- soft side-glance
- compact sitting
- floppy lying-down poses
- calm curiosity
- mild surprise rather than exaggerated sharpness

Strong acting is allowed, but the otter should not become sly, hyper-intelligent or aggressive.

---

The approved acting stress test confirms identity retention while:

- standing
- walking
- running
- jumping
- sitting
- lying down
- sleeping
- peeking
- reading
- using a phone
- drinking
- eating
- swimming / floating
- wearing rain clothing
- comforting

- body flattening while lying down
- tail bending and trailing
- mild face compression
- larger arm gestures

Observed drift to avoid:

- whiskers becoming too prominent
- muzzle becoming too animal-like
- nose/mouth becoming overly detailed
- surface texture becoming too furry or sketchy
- body becoming too tall or slim

---

## 16. Prohibited Drift

Reject generations where the otter:

- becomes tall or slim
- develops a long realistic muzzle
- gains large ears
- gains long limbs
- loses the cream belly / face region
- develops realistic fur
- receives tiny black-dot eyes
- becomes sly or fox-like
- gains a long thin tail
- becomes glossy 3D
- becomes a plastic mascot
- becomes photorealistic
- changes into another species
