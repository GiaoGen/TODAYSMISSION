# TODAYSMISSION Character Prompt Fragment
## Character 05 — Otter

Version: 1.0
Status: LOCKED / PRODUCTION READY

---

## Character Identity

Use the canonical TODAYSMISSION Character 05 — Otter.

Preserve the established identity exactly.

The character is:

- a short, compact, rounded cocoa-brown otter
- large warm-cream belly / lower-face region
- very small round ears
- large white eyes
- simple dark movable pupils
- tiny nose and minimal mouth
- short thick limbs
- thick soft tapered tail
- slightly blank, slow-to-react, gentle emotional presence
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation style

The character must remain immediately recognizable as the same TODAYSMISSION otter.

---

## Face / Eye Identity

Preserve:

- clean broad cream lower-face area
- large readable white eyes
- simple movable pupils
- tiny simple nose
- minimal mouth
- very small ears

Whiskers, if present, must remain subtle.

Do not add:

- realistic muzzle anatomy
- strong whisker pads
- detailed nostrils
- tiny black-dot eyes
- anime eyes
- glossy kawaii highlights

---

## Body Identity

Preserve:

- short compact proportions
- rounded body
- large cream belly
- short thick limbs
- thick naturally attached tail
- low center of gravity

Do not make the otter:

- tall
- slim
- long-limbed
- long-necked
- anatomically realistic

---

## Tail Identity

Preserve:

- thick soft form
- tapered rounded shape
- natural attachment to the body

The tail may bend, flatten, trail or move freely.

Do not make it thin, long, whip-like or sharply pointed.

---

## Rendering Language

Use the established TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- subtle line-weight variation
- matte flat colors
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-style tonal variation
- finish quality matching the Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- rough sketch rendering
- overly furry texture
- glossy AI mascot rendering
- perfect vector perfection
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- smooth synthetic gradients
- photorealism

---

## Acting Freedom

Preserve identity, not rigid geometry.

The character may freely change:

- pose
- expression
- pupil direction
- eye openness
- moderate eye size
- mouth shape
- limb placement
- tail position
- body tilt
- squash and stretch
- swimming posture
- camera angle

Strong acting is allowed, but preserve the otter's gentle blankness.

---

## Emotional Direction

The otter's core emotional quality is:

- adorably blank
- gentle
- laid-back
- innocent
- calm
- slightly slow to react

Do not turn the otter into a sly, sharp or hyperactive character.

---

## Accessories and Scene Freedom

Accessories, clothing and props may be introduced when appropriate.

They must never obscure or redesign the character's core identity.

The scene may freely determine:

- environment
- composition
- camera
- clothing
- accessories
- props
- lighting
- storytelling

---

## Hard Restrictions

Never:

- create a strong realistic muzzle
- enlarge the ears
- lengthen the limbs
- make the character tall or slim
- replace the eyes with tiny black dots
- remove the cream belly / lower-face region
- make the tail thin or whip-like
- make the surface heavily furry
- switch to glossy 3D mascot rendering
- redesign the character

---

## Production Principle

Character 05 is already designed.

Do NOT invent or reinterpret its appearance.

Spend creative freedom on:

ACTING
SCENE
STORYTELLING
COMPOSITION

while keeping the character identity stable.
