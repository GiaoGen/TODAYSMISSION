# TODAYSMISSION Character Prompt Fragment
## Character 01 — Dinosaur

Version: 1.0
Status: LOCKED / PRODUCTION READY

---

## Character Identity

Use the canonical TODAYSMISSION Character 01 — Dinosaur.

Preserve the established character identity exactly.

The character is:

- a soft, compact, chubby dinosaur
- Tiffany-blue body
- large warm-cream belly
- deep-teal rounded dorsal plates
- short thick upward-curved tail
- short thick soft limbs
- large close-set white eyes
- simple dark movable pupils
- no visible nose
- integrated head-and-body silhouette
- low center of gravity
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation style

The character must remain immediately recognizable as the same TODAYSMISSION dinosaur.

---

## Eye Identity

The large close-set eyes are a major identity feature.

Preserve:

- large white eye areas
- very narrow spacing between the eyes
- simple dark pupils
- strong readable gaze

Do not replace them with tiny black-dot eyes.

Do not add:

- detailed irises
- anime eye rendering
- eyelashes
- glossy kawaii highlights

Eyes may deform freely during acting.

---

## Body Identity

Preserve the dinosaur's:

- wide rounded body
- chubby proportions
- large rounded belly
- short arms
- short legs
- soft rounded dorsal plates
- short chunky tail

Do not make the dinosaur:

- tall
- slim
- long-necked
- anatomically realistic
- sharp or reptilian

---

## Rendering Language

Use the established TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- subtle natural imperfection
- subtle line-weight variation
- matte flat colors
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-style tonal variation
- finish quality matching the approved Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- raw sketch rendering
- glossy AI mascot rendering
- polished vector perfection
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- smooth synthetic gradients
- photorealism

---

## Acting Freedom

Preserve identity, not rigid geometry.

The character may freely change:

- pose
- expression
- pupil direction
- eye openness
- eye shape
- mouth size and shape
- eyebrows
- body tilt
- limb placement
- weight distribution
- squash and stretch
- camera angle

Strong acting and asymmetry are encouraged.

The character should behave like an animation performer, not a static sticker.

---

## Accessories and Scene Freedom

Accessories, clothing and props may be introduced when appropriate.

They must never obscure or redesign the character's core identity.

The scene may freely determine:

- environment
- camera
- composition
- clothing
- accessories
- props
- lighting
- storytelling

---

## Hard Restrictions

Never:

- add a visible nose or nostrils
- separate the eyes widely
- replace the eyes with tiny black dots
- remove the cream belly
- remove the dorsal plates
- turn the dorsal plates into sharp spikes
- lengthen the limbs dramatically
- create a long thin tail
- redesign the character
- reinterpret the character as another dinosaur design
- switch to glossy 3D mascot rendering

---

## Production Principle

Character 01 is already designed.

Do NOT invent or redesign its appearance.

Spend creative freedom on:

ACTING  
SCENE  
STORYTELLING  
COMPOSITION

while keeping the character identity stable.
