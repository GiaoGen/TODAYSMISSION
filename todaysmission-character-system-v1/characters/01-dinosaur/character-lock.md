# TODAYSMISSION Character Lock
## Character 01 — Dinosaur

Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0
Role: Production Character

---

## 1. Identity

Character 01 is a soft, chubby dinosaur belonging to the TODAYSMISSION character universe.

Its visual identity is defined by:

- Tiffany-blue body
- large cream belly
- rounded compact silhouette
- large close-set white eyes
- dark movable pupils
- no visible nose
- rounded dorsal plates
- short chunky tail
- short thick limbs
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation rendering

The character must remain immediately recognizable across poses, expressions, scenes, camera angles, clothing and accessories.

---

## 2. Core Silhouette

The dinosaur has a compact, low-center-of-gravity body.

The overall body should feel:

- round
- wide
- soft
- chubby
- slightly vertically extended
- never perfectly spherical

Avoid:

- tall slim body
- narrow torso
- long neck
- large separate head
- realistic dinosaur anatomy

The head and torso visually form one continuous body mass.

---

## 3. Body Proportions

The dinosaur should remain visibly wide and compact.

General relationship:

- width approximately 85–95% of standing height
- large central body mass
- short legs
- short arms
- low visual center of gravity

Exact geometry may deform during acting.

These proportions describe the neutral canonical state, not a rigid pose constraint.

---

## 4. Color Identity

### Body
Tiffany-blue / turquoise family.

Canonical direction: approximately `#7DD3C7`

### Belly
Warm cream.

Canonical direction: approximately `#F6EBD7`

### Dorsal plates
Deep teal.

Canonical direction: approximately `#3A9CA8`

### Linework
Dark desaturated teal / charcoal-teal.

Do not use harsh pure-black outlines as the default.

Minor variation caused by texture, lighting or reproduction is acceptable.

---

## 5. Eyes

The eyes are one of the dinosaur's strongest identity features.

Required characteristics:

- large white eye areas
- very close spacing
- simple dark pupils
- substantial pupil movement space
- highly readable gaze direction
- slightly imperfect hand-drawn geometry

The two eyes should feel visually connected as one expressive eye system.

They should not appear as two widely separated eyes.

### Allowed during acting

Eyes may:

- widen
- compress
- squint
- close
- become asymmetric
- shift vertically
- slightly change size
- rotate with head/body acting

Pupils may:

- move strongly toward edges
- converge
- diverge slightly
- shrink when startled
- change position dramatically

### Forbidden

Do not introduce:

- detailed irises
- anime eyes
- eyelashes
- glossy kawaii highlights
- realistic eye rendering
- permanently tiny black-dot eyes

---

## 6. Nose

The dinosaur has no visible nose.

Do not add:

- nostrils
- snout markings
- realistic muzzle anatomy
- a separate nose shape

This is an intentional identity feature.

---

## 7. Mouth

Neutral mouth treatment is minimal.

During acting, the mouth is highly elastic.

Allowed:

- tiny neutral curve
- compressed nervous mouth
- asymmetric mouth
- wide smile
- open laugh
- large shocked mouth
- determined mouth shapes

The mouth may change significantly in size when acting.

Do not use realistic lips or detailed dental anatomy.

---

## 8. Dorsal Plates

Rounded dorsal plates run from the top/back of the head toward the tail.

Visual rules:

- rounded rather than sharp
- thick and soft
- deep teal
- progressively integrated into the body
- consistent rhythmic spacing

The plates are a permanent silhouette feature.

They must never become:

- sharp reptilian spikes
- thin triangular spikes
- realistic scales

Minor perspective overlap is expected.

Apparent plate count may change because of angle or occlusion, but the underlying arrangement must remain consistent.

---

## 9. Tail

The tail is:

- short
- thick
- soft
- slightly upward-curved
- visually integrated into the body

The tail should never become:

- long
- thin
- whip-like
- sharply pointed
- realistic reptilian anatomy

Tail position may move freely during acting.

---

## 10. Arms and Legs

Limbs are:

- short
- thick
- rounded
- soft
- simple

Hands do not use realistic fingers.

Feet remain simplified.

Limb geometry may stretch or compress during expressive acting, but should return to the canonical short, chubby structure.

---

## 11. Belly

The cream belly is a major visual anchor.

It should feel:

- large
- soft
- rounded
- slightly irregular
- integrated into the body

Avoid a narrow vertical oval.

The belly may deform naturally with:

- perspective
- sitting
- bending
- squash/stretch
- body rotation

Do not redesign its basic placement or overall scale.

---

## 12. Rendering Language

Use the TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style.

Required:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- subtle natural imperfection
- slight line-weight variation
- matte flat dominant color areas
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-like tonal variation
- finish quality matching the approved Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- raw sketch rendering
- glossy mascot rendering
- plastic material
- 3D toy appearance
- smooth AI gradients
- perfect vector geometry
- heavy watercolor texture
- photorealistic skin
- thick uniform black outlines

The result should feel human-drawn but clean enough for product UI.

## 14. Elastic Acting Zone

These properties are intentionally NOT locked:

- pose
- facial expression
- pupil direction
- eye openness
- slight eye size changes
- mouth size
- mouth shape
- eyebrows
- limb placement
- body tilt
- head/body angle
- weight distribution
- squash and stretch
- posture
- camera angle

Accessories, clothing and props may also change freely.

Core principle:

> Preserve identity, not rigid geometry.

---

## 15. Acting Rules

The character must remain capable of strong animation-style acting.

Strong emotional scenes may use:

- asymmetry
- body lean
- compression
- extension
- dramatic gaze
- uneven arm placement
- uneven leg placement
- large mouth deformation
- eye deformation

Do not reduce the character to:

"same neutral body + different facial icon."

Body and face should participate together in acting.

---

## 16. Stress-Test Findings

The approved acting stress test confirms identity retention across:

- awkward arrival
- trying to act normal
- nervousness
- surprise
- happiness
- determination
- sadness
- shyness
- thinking
- relaxation
- excitement
- pride
- frustration
- curiosity
- comfort

Observed acceptable elastic changes:

- large pose changes
- body compression / flattening
- eye widening and narrowing
- strong gaze shifts
- sitting and lying-down deformations
- large mouth deformation

Observed drift to avoid:

- eyes shrinking into generic small kawaii eyes
- dorsal plate rhythm changing too much
- belly being redesigned per pose
- body becoming tall or slim

---

## 17. Prohibited Drift

Reject generations where the dinosaur:

- becomes tall or slim
- gains a visible nose
- develops a realistic dinosaur muzzle
- loses its cream belly
- loses dorsal plates
- gains sharp spikes
- develops long limbs
- develops a long thin tail
- receives widely separated eyes
- switches to tiny black-dot eyes
- becomes glossy 3D
- becomes a plastic mascot
- becomes photorealistic
- changes into another character species

---

When generating future artwork, reference priority is:

1. Approved Dinosaur Canonical Model

It must NOT be treated as a fixed pose library.
