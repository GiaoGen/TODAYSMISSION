# TODAYSMISSION Character Lock
## Character 06 — Rabbit

Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0
Role: Production Character

---

## 1. Identity

Character 06 is a quick-minded, observant rabbit belonging to the TODAYSMISSION character universe.

Its identity is defined by:

- warm-gray rounded body
- warm-cream muzzle / belly region
- long rounded ears with soft pink inner ears
- small-to-medium white eyes
- simple dark movable pupils
- short thick limbs
- small round fluffy tail
- compact low-center proportions
- smart, alert and friendly emotional presence
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation rendering

The character must remain immediately recognizable across poses, expressions, scenes, clothing, accessories and camera angles.

---

## 2. Character Role

The rabbit is the smart, quick-minded and observant member of the family.

Core personality:

- smart
- observant
- quick-minded
- curious
- lively
- friendly
- responsive

Shyness may appear as an emotion, but it is NOT the rabbit's defining personality.

The rabbit should feel bright and alert rather than timid, passive or baby-like.

---

## 3. Core Silhouette

The rabbit has a compact, rounded body with long ears.

Required:

- short overall stature
- rounded torso
- short thick limbs
- long soft ears
- small round tail
- low center of gravity
- clean readable silhouette

Avoid:

- tall slim rabbit anatomy
- long legs
- narrow torso
- realistic hare anatomy
- oversized baby-rabbit head
- droopy-lop-rabbit identity

---

## 4. Eyes

The rabbit uses a small-to-medium white-eye system designed for quick readable gaze.

Required:

- clear white eye area
- small dark movable pupils
- medium spacing
- alert directional gaze
- soft rounded geometry
- strong side-eye / observation capability

Allowed during acting:

- widening
- compression
- asymmetry
- fast directional gaze
- half-lidding
- full closure
- modest size changes

Avoid:

- tiny black-dot eyes
- huge generic kawaii eyes
- anime irises
- glossy eye highlights
- eyelashes

The gaze should communicate intelligence, quick reaction and curiosity.

---

## 5. Ears

The ears are a major acting feature.

Canonical structure:

- long
- rounded
- soft
- gray outer ear
- warm pink inner ear
- upright / lightly angled in neutral states

During acting, ears may:

- tilt
- rotate
- bend
- flatten
- briefly droop
- become asymmetric

However, the rabbit must not permanently become a lop-eared rabbit.

Ear motion is expressive deformation, not species redesign.

---

## 6. Face

The face remains simple.

Required:

- small restrained nose / mouth
- warm-cream muzzle area
- uncluttered facial design
- optional subtle brow acting

Avoid:

- realistic muzzle anatomy
- pronounced snout
- detailed nostrils
- permanent blush marks
- detailed lips or teeth

Blush may appear temporarily as an emotional effect but is not a locked identity feature.

---

## 7. Belly

The warm-cream belly is a major visual anchor.

It should remain:

- large enough to read clearly
- rounded
- soft
- integrated into the torso
- visually simple

It may deform naturally during sitting, running, jumping, lying down and squash/stretch.

---

## 8. Limbs

Arms and legs are:

- short
- thick
- rounded
- simple

Hands and feet remain simplified.

Limb geometry may stretch or compress during acting but should return to the canonical compact proportions.

---

## 9. Tail

The tail is:

- small
- round
- fluffy
- warm cream
- positioned high on the rear body

It should remain secondary to the ears and body silhouette.

Do not enlarge the tail into a dominant feature.

## 11. Rendering Language

Use the TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style.

Required:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- subtle natural imperfection
- controlled line-weight variation
- matte flat color areas
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-like tonal variation
- finish quality matching the Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- raw sketch rendering
- heavy fur texture
- glossy AI mascot rendering
- perfect vector geometry
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- smooth synthetic gradients
- photorealism

## 14. Acting Rules

The rabbit should act like a quick, alert animation performer.

Useful acting tendencies include:

- fast side-glance
- quick head turn
- asymmetrical ears
- one ear reacting before the other
- forward lean
- curious posture
- quick compact movement
- alert listening
- subtle confident determination

Strong acting is encouraged, but the rabbit should not become frantic, childish or permanently shy.

## 16. Prohibited Drift

Reject generations where the rabbit:

- becomes white instead of warm gray
- loses the cream muzzle / belly
- loses the pink inner ears
- becomes permanently lop-eared
- develops long realistic legs
- becomes tall or slim
- switches to black-dot eyes
- receives giant generic kawaii eyes
- develops realistic rabbit fur
- becomes glossy 3D
- becomes a plastic mascot
- becomes photorealistic
- loses its smart, observant personality
- changes into another species
