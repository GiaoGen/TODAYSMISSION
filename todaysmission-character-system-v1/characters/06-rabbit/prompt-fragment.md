# TODAYSMISSION Character Prompt Fragment
## Character 06 — Rabbit

Version: 1.0
Status: LOCKED / PRODUCTION READY

---

## Character Identity

Use the canonical TODAYSMISSION Character 06 — Rabbit.

Preserve the established identity exactly.

The character is:

- a compact rounded warm-gray rabbit
- warm-cream muzzle and belly
- long rounded ears
- soft pink inner ears
- small-to-medium white eyes
- simple dark movable pupils
- short thick limbs
- small round fluffy tail
- smart, observant, quick-minded emotional presence
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation style

The character must remain immediately recognizable as the same TODAYSMISSION rabbit.

---

## Eye Identity

Preserve:

- small-to-medium white eye areas
- dark movable pupils
- alert readable gaze
- soft rounded geometry

The rabbit's eyes should support quick observation, side-glance and fast reaction.

Do not replace them with:

- tiny black dots
- huge generic kawaii eyes
- anime eyes
- glossy eye rendering

---

## Ear Identity

Preserve:

- long rounded ears
- warm-gray outer ears
- soft pink inner ears

Neutral ears are mostly upright or lightly angled.

During acting, ears may tilt, bend, flatten, briefly droop or become asymmetric.

Do NOT permanently redesign the rabbit as lop-eared.

---

## Body Identity

Preserve:

- compact short proportions
- rounded torso
- cream belly
- short thick limbs
- small round tail
- low center of gravity

Do not make the rabbit:

- tall
- slim
- long-legged
- anatomically realistic
- baby-like generic mascot

---

## Rendering Language

Use the established TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- subtle line-weight variation
- matte flat colors
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-style tonal variation
- finish quality matching the Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- rough sketch rendering
- realistic fur
- glossy AI mascot rendering
- perfect vector perfection
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- smooth synthetic gradients
- photorealism

---

## Acting Freedom

Preserve identity, not rigid geometry.

The character may freely change:

- pose
- expression
- pupil direction
- eye openness
- modest eye size
- mouth shape
- brow acting
- ear angle
- temporary ear bend / droop
- limb placement
- body tilt
- squash and stretch
- camera angle

Strong acting and asymmetry are encouraged.

---

## Emotional Direction

The rabbit's core emotional quality is:

- smart
- observant
- quick-minded
- curious
- lively
- friendly

Shyness is an available emotion, not the defining personality.

---

## Accessories and Scene Freedom

Accessories, clothing and props may be introduced when appropriate.

They must never obscure or redesign the character's core identity.

The scene may freely determine:

- environment
- composition
- camera
- clothing
- accessories
- props
- lighting
- storytelling

---

## Hard Restrictions

Never:

- change the body from warm gray to white
- remove the cream muzzle / belly
- remove the pink inner ears
- permanently make the ears droop
- turn the rabbit into a lop-eared breed
- enlarge the eyes into generic kawaii eyes
- replace the eyes with black dots
- make the body tall or slim
- make blush a permanent facial marking
- switch to glossy 3D mascot rendering
- redesign the character

---

## Production Principle

Character 06 is already designed.

Do NOT invent or reinterpret its appearance.

Spend creative freedom on:

ACTING
SCENE
STORYTELLING
COMPOSITION

while keeping the character identity stable.
