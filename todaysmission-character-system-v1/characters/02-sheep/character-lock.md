# TODAYSMISSION Character Lock
## Character 02 — Sheep

Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0
Role: Production Character

---

## 1. Identity

Character 02 is a soft, warm, emotionally reassuring sheep belonging to the TODAYSMISSION character universe.

Its visual identity is defined by:

- warm cream wool
- deep warm-gray face plate
- soft drooping ears
- compact rounded silhouette
- medium white eyes with dark movable pupils
- short thick limbs
- very small round tail
- gentle, supportive emotional presence
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation rendering

The character must remain immediately recognizable across poses, expressions, scenes, camera angles, clothing and accessories.

---

## 2. Character Role

The sheep is the gentle emotional anchor of the character family.

Core personality:

- gentle
- empathetic
- supportive
- reassuring
- warm
- slightly shy
- emotionally attentive

The sheep should feel comforting rather than weak, passive, or sleepy.

---

## 3. Core Silhouette

The sheep has a compact, rounded, low-center-of-gravity body.

The overall silhouette should feel:

- soft
- fluffy
- rounded
- vertically compact
- slightly cloud-like
- friendly and stable

Avoid:

- tall slim proportions
- realistic sheep anatomy
- long neck
- narrow torso
- excessively shaggy wool
- highly detailed curls

The wool silhouette should remain graphic and controlled.

---

## 4. Wool

The wool is a major identity feature.

Required characteristics:

- warm cream color
- rounded cloud-like outer contour
- simplified clumps
- clean graphic silhouette
- soft but controlled texture
- no realistic fur rendering

The wool may compress or stretch naturally during acting.

Do not let the wool become:

- photorealistic
- overly fluffy
- messy
- highly curly
- shaggy
- rough pencil fuzz

The wool should remain refined and product-ready.

---

## 5. Face Plate

The face plate is deep warm gray.

It should:

- remain clearly visible inside the cream wool
- stay simple and rounded
- provide strong contrast for the eyes
- remain a permanent identity feature

The face plate must not become:

- pure black
- sharply geometric
- realistic sheep facial anatomy
- overly large
- too small to read

---

## 6. Eyes

The eyes are medium-sized white eyes with dark movable pupils.

Required:

- clear white eye area
- dark simple pupils
- readable gaze direction
- soft rounded geometry
- enough room for expressive pupil movement

The eye system should support:

- gentle concern
- curiosity
- warmth
- shyness
- surprise
- reassurance
- determination

Allowed during acting:

- widening
- compression
- slight asymmetry
- closing
- squinting
- directional gaze
- modest size variation

Avoid:

- tiny black-dot eyes
- anime eyes
- detailed irises
- eyelashes
- glossy kawaii highlights
- exaggerated eye enlargement that changes identity

---

## 7. Nose and Mouth

The sheep may use extremely minimal nose/mouth information.

Default treatment:

- tiny simple mouth
- minimal or near-invisible nose
- no realistic muzzle

The mouth may become more expressive during acting.

Do not introduce:

- detailed nostrils
- realistic sheep muzzle anatomy
- strong snout projection
- detailed lips or teeth

---

## 8. Ears

The ears are:

- soft
- rounded
- drooping
- warm gray/brown
- visually integrated into the wool

They should remain relatively short and simple.

Allowed:

- slight lift
- tilt
- compression
- asymmetry during acting

Avoid:

- long rabbit-like ears
- pointed ears
- realistic ear anatomy

---

## 9. Limbs

Arms and legs are:

- short
- thick
- rounded
- simple

Hands do not use realistic fingers.

Feet remain compact and simplified.

Limb geometry may stretch or compress during acting, but should return to the canonical short and soft structure.

---

## 10. Tail

The tail is:

- small
- round
- fluffy
- cream-colored
- visually secondary

It should never become a large or dominant silhouette feature.

## 12. Rendering Language

Use the TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style.

Required:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- slight natural imperfection
- subtle line-weight variation
- matte flat color areas
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-style tonal variation
- refined finish level matching the Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- raw sketchiness
- overly rough pencil texture
- glossy mascot rendering
- perfect vector geometry
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- smooth synthetic gradients
- realistic fur

Hand-drawn roughness should remain subtle and controlled.

## 15. Acting Rules

The sheep should act like an animation performer, not a static sticker.

Strong emotional scenes may use:

- asymmetry
- inward body compression
- slight body lean
- expressive gaze
- hands held near the chest
- open supportive gestures
- ear movement
- sitting, running, jumping, lying down, peeking or hiding

The sheep should retain a gentle emotional core even during strong acting.

Do not reduce the character to:

"same neutral body + different facial icon."

---

## 16. Stress-Test Findings

The approved acting stress test confirms that the sheep can remain recognizable while:

- standing
- sitting
- walking
- running
- jumping
- lying down
- sleeping
- hiding
- peeking
- reading
- using a phone
- drinking
- eating
- comforting
- encouraging

Observed acceptable elastic changes:

- temporary eye enlargement
- wool compression
- body flattening while lying down
- stronger facial asymmetry during emotion

Observed drift to avoid:

- overly fluffy wool
- rougher rendering than the canonical model
- overdefined nose/mouth
- eye enlargement that changes the face architecture

---

## 17. Prohibited Drift

Reject generations where the sheep:

- becomes tall or slim
- loses the cream wool
- loses the warm-gray face plate
- develops realistic wool or fur
- gains long or pointed ears
- receives rabbit-like anatomy
- develops a realistic muzzle
- switches to tiny black-dot eyes
- becomes glossy 3D
- becomes a plastic mascot
- becomes photorealistic
- loses its gentle identity
- changes into another character species

---
