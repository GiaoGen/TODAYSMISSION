# TODAYSMISSION Character Prompt Fragment
## Character 02 — Sheep

Version: 1.0
Status: LOCKED / PRODUCTION READY

---

## Character Identity

Use the canonical TODAYSMISSION Character 02 — Sheep.

Preserve the established identity exactly.

The character is:

- a compact, soft, rounded sheep
- warm cream wool
- deep warm-gray face plate
- soft drooping warm-gray ears
- medium white eyes
- simple dark movable pupils
- short thick limbs
- small round fluffy tail
- gentle, supportive emotional presence
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation style

The character must remain immediately recognizable as the same TODAYSMISSION sheep.

---

## Eye Identity

Preserve:

- medium white eye areas
- simple dark pupils
- readable gaze direction
- soft rounded geometry

Eyes may deform naturally during acting.

Do not replace them with tiny black-dot eyes.

Do not add:

- detailed irises
- anime eye rendering
- eyelashes
- glossy kawaii highlights

---

## Body Identity

Preserve the sheep's:

- compact rounded body
- controlled cloud-like wool silhouette
- deep warm-gray face plate
- short thick limbs
- soft drooping ears
- small fluffy tail

Do not make the sheep:

- tall
- slim
- long-necked
- anatomically realistic
- overly shaggy
- rabbit-like

---

## Rendering Language

Use the established TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- slight natural imperfection
- subtle line-weight variation
- matte flat colors
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-style tonal variation
- finish quality matching the Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- rough sketch rendering
- overly fuzzy wool texture
- polished vector perfection
- glossy AI mascot rendering
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- smooth synthetic gradients
- photorealism

---

## Acting Freedom

Preserve identity, not rigid geometry.

The character may freely change:

- pose
- expression
- pupil direction
- eye openness
- modest eye size
- mouth shape
- eyebrows
- ear angle
- limb placement
- body tilt
- squash and stretch
- camera angle

Strong acting and asymmetry are allowed.

The character should behave like an animation performer, not a static sticker.

---

## Emotional Direction

The sheep's core emotional quality is:

- gentle
- empathetic
- supportive
- warm
- slightly shy
- emotionally attentive

Even in strong emotional scenes, preserve this gentle emotional identity.

---

## Accessories and Scene Freedom

Accessories, clothing and props may be introduced when appropriate.

They must never obscure or redesign the character's core identity.

The scene may freely determine:

- environment
- camera
- composition
- clothing
- accessories
- props
- lighting
- storytelling

---

## Hard Restrictions

Never:

- remove the cream wool
- remove the warm-gray face plate
- add realistic sheep anatomy
- add a strong muzzle
- add long or pointed ears
- turn the character into a rabbit
- replace the eyes with tiny black dots
- make the wool highly realistic or shaggy
- make the character tall or slim
- switch to glossy 3D mascot rendering
- redesign the character

---

## Production Principle

Character 02 is already designed.

Do NOT invent or reinterpret its appearance.

Spend creative freedom on:

ACTING  
SCENE  
STORYTELLING  
COMPOSITION

while keeping the character identity stable.
