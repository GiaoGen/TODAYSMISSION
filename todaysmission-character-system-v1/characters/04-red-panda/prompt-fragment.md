# TODAYSMISSION Character Prompt Fragment
## Character 04 — Red Panda

Version: 1.0
Status: LOCKED / PRODUCTION READY

---

## Character Identity

Use the canonical TODAYSMISSION Character 04 — Red Panda.

Preserve the established identity exactly.

The character is:

- a short, compact, soft red panda
- burnt-orange body
- visually large rounded head
- restrained warm-cream facial color blocking
- small-to-medium white expressive eyes
- simple dark movable pupils
- tiny nose and mouth
- short thick dark-brown paws and feet
- oversized fluffy striped tail
- naturally shy, warm emotional presence
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation style

The character must remain immediately recognizable as the same TODAYSMISSION red panda.

---

## Face / Eye Identity

Preserve:

- simplified cream cheek / eye-area markings
- small-to-medium white eyes
- dark movable pupils
- eyes relatively centered on the face
- tiny nose and minimal mouth

Do not expand the facial markings into a realistic red-panda mask.

Do not replace the eyes with:

- tiny black dots
- huge protagonist-style eyes
- anime eyes
- glossy kawaii eyes

---

## Body Identity

Preserve:

- short compact proportions
- round torso
- visually large head
- short thick limbs
- dark paws
- large naturally attached striped tail

Do not make the character:

- tall
- slim
- long-limbed
- fox-like
- anatomically realistic

---

## Tail Identity

The tail is a key identity feature.

Preserve:

- large size
- fluffy rounded form
- orange / brown stripes
- natural attachment to the body

The tail may move, curve, compress or flatten during acting.

Do not make it thin, tiny, detached or fan-like.

---

## Rendering Identity

Use the established TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style:

- delicate, lightweight hand-drawn contours
- subtle, non-uniform line-weight variation
- soft contour transitions with restrained edge contrast
- refined matte color blocking
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-style tonal variation
- clear color shapes with texture secondary
- character edges integrated naturally into the illustrated environment
- finish quality matching the approved Canonical Model system, especially the cleaner refined standard established after the Dinosaur cleanup phase

Avoid:

- thick dark outlines around the full silhouette
- a bold contour enclosing the character like a sticker
- heavy uniform line thickness
- coarse edge treatment
- rough heavy comic outlines
- cutout or pasted-on mascot appearance
- rough sketch rendering
- overly fuzzy fur
- over-texturing or noisy fur texture
- glossy AI mascot rendering
- perfect vector perfection
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- smooth synthetic gradients
- photorealism

The outline must support the red panda's shape, not dominate the image.

---

## Acting Freedom

Preserve identity, not rigid geometry.

The character may freely change:

- pose
- expression
- pupil direction
- eye openness
- modest eye size
- mouth shape
- paw placement
- tail position
- body tilt
- squash and stretch
- camera angle

Strong acting and asymmetry are allowed.

---

## Emotional Direction

The red panda's core emotional quality is:

- warm
- shy
- gentle
- sensitive
- soft
- comforting

Even in dynamic scenes, preserve this emotional identity.

---

## Accessories and Scene Freedom

Accessories, clothing and props may be introduced when appropriate.

They must never obscure or redesign the character's core identity.

The scene may freely determine:

- environment
- composition
- camera
- clothing
- accessories
- props
- lighting
- storytelling

---

## Hard Restrictions

Never:

- remove the large striped tail
- expand the cream facial markings into a realistic mask
- enlarge the eyes into protagonist-scale eyes
- replace the eyes with black dots
- add a strong realistic muzzle
- make the character tall or slim
- add long limbs
- switch to realistic fur
- switch to glossy 3D mascot rendering
- redesign the character

---

## Production Principle

Character 04 is already designed.

Do NOT invent or reinterpret its appearance.

Spend creative freedom on:

ACTING
SCENE
STORYTELLING
COMPOSITION

while keeping the character identity stable.
