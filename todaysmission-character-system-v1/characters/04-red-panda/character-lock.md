# TODAYSMISSION Character Lock
## Character 04 — Red Panda

Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0
Role: Production Character

---

## 1. Identity

Character 04 is a soft, shy, warm red panda belonging to the TODAYSMISSION character universe.

Its identity is defined by:

- burnt-orange rounded body
- compact short proportions
- visually large head relative to body
- simplified restrained cream facial markings
- small-to-medium white eyes
- simple dark movable pupils
- very small nose and mouth
- dark brown short thick paws and feet
- large fluffy striped tail
- inward, slightly self-protective default hand placement
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation rendering

The character must remain immediately recognizable across poses, expressions, scenes, clothing and camera angles.

---

## 2. Character Role

The red panda is the warm, shy and sensitive member of the character family.

Core personality:

- warm
- shy
- gentle
- sensitive
- soft
- comforting
- slightly self-conscious

The red panda should feel emotionally approachable and lovable without becoming baby-like or generic.

---

## 3. Core Silhouette

The red panda has a short, compact, rounded body.

Required:

- short overall stature
- round compact torso
- visually large head
- short thick limbs
- large striped tail
- low center of gravity

Avoid:

- tall or slim proportions
- long torso
- realistic red-panda anatomy
- generic upright mascot proportions
- narrow body

The silhouette should feel soft, compressed and slightly inward.

---

## 4. Face Pattern

The simplified cream facial color blocking is a major identity feature.

Required:

- restrained cream cheek / eye-area markings
- small cream muzzle area
- clear but simple geometry
- warm, soft expression
- markings that support identity without becoming realistic

Do not expand the cream markings into:

- a full realistic red-panda mask
- highly detailed fur markings
- complex symmetrical facial striping
- large cream areas that dominate the face

The face pattern should remain simplified and graphic.

---

## 5. Eyes

The red panda uses small-to-medium expressive white eyes.

Required:

- visible white eye area
- simple dark movable pupils
- eyes placed relatively toward the center of the face
- gentle readable gaze
- soft rounded geometry

Allowed during acting:

- modest widening
- compression
- slight asymmetry
- directional gaze
- closing
- squinting
- temporary size variation

Avoid:

- tiny black-dot eyes
- huge protagonist-style eyes
- anime eyes
- detailed irises
- glossy kawaii highlights
- eyelashes

The eyes should remain expressive without becoming the dominant feature.

---

## 6. Nose and Mouth

The nose and mouth are intentionally small.

Required:

- tiny simple dark nose
- minimal mouth
- soft muzzle treatment
- no strong snout projection

Avoid:

- realistic muzzle anatomy
- large nose
- pronounced snout
- detailed lips or teeth

The nose/mouth must remain secondary to the eyes and face markings.

---

## 7. Ears

The ears are:

- short
- rounded
- soft
- lightly cream inside
- integrated into the head silhouette

Avoid:

- tall pointed ears
- fox-like sharp ears
- realistic ear anatomy

---

## 8. Paws and Limbs

The forepaws and feet are deep warm brown.

Required:

- short
- thick
- rounded
- soft
- visually simple

A slightly inward hand placement near the chest is a signature neutral tendency, especially in shy or uncertain states.

The paws may move freely during acting.

Do not lock every pose to the inward-paw position.

---

## 9. Tail

The tail is one of the strongest identity features.

Required:

- large
- fluffy
- rounded
- naturally attached to the body
- orange / brown stripe rhythm
- strong silhouette contribution

The tail may:

- curve
- compress
- lie down
- swing
- partially hide behind the body
- flatten under perspective

It must never become:

- thin
- whip-like
- tiny
- realistic
- disconnected from the body
- a separate fan-like prop

The striped pattern should remain recognizable.

## 11. Rendering Identity

Use the TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style.

Required:

- delicate, lightweight hand-drawn contours
- subtle, non-uniform line-weight variation
- soft contour transitions with restrained edge contrast
- subtle natural imperfection
- refined matte color blocks
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-like tonal variation
- the character visually integrated into the illustrated environment
- finish quality matching the approved Canonical Model system, especially the cleaner refined standard established after the Dinosaur cleanup phase

Avoid:

- a thick dark outline around the full silhouette
- a bold contour enclosing the character like a sticker
- heavy uniform line thickness
- coarse edge treatment
- rough heavy comic outlines
- a cutout or pasted-on mascot appearance
- raw sketchiness
- overly fuzzy fur
- over-texturing or noisy fur texture
- realistic fur rendering
- glossy mascot rendering
- perfect vector geometry
- 3D toy aesthetics
- smooth synthetic gradients
- photorealism

Hand-drawn texture must remain subtle and refined. Clear color shapes stay primary, and the outline supports the form without dominating it.

- mouth shape
- brow / upper-eye acting
- limb placement
- tail position
- body tilt
- squash and stretch
- sitting / running / jumping / lying down / peeking
- camera angle
- props
- clothing
- accessories

Core principle:

> Preserve identity, not rigid geometry.

---

## 14. Acting Rules

The red panda should act like a soft, emotionally sensitive animation performer.

Useful acting tendencies include:

- paws drawn inward
- slight body compression
- shy side-glance
- looking away
- subtle asymmetry
- tail responding to mood
- sitting compactly
- curling inward
- gentle comforting gestures
- small but readable gaze changes

Strong acting is allowed, but the character should not become aggressive, hyperactive, or overly heroic by default.

## 16. Prohibited Drift

Reject generations where the red panda:

- becomes tall or slim
- loses the large striped tail
- loses the restrained cream face markings
- gains realistic red-panda fur
- develops a strong snout
- receives huge protagonist-style eyes
- receives tiny black-dot eyes
- develops long limbs
- becomes fox-like
- becomes glossy 3D
- becomes a plastic mascot
- becomes photorealistic
- loses the shy warm personality
- changes into another species
