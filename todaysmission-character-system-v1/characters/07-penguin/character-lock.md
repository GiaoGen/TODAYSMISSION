# TODAYSMISSION Character Lock
## Character 07 — Penguin

Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0
Role: Production Character

---

## 1. Identity

Character 07 is a calm, subtle, quietly expressive penguin belonging to the TODAYSMISSION character universe.

Its identity is defined by:

- deep navy rounded body
- warm-cream face / belly region
- compact pear-shaped silhouette
- low center of gravity
- short rounded flippers
- small warm-orange beak
- small warm-orange feet
- white eyes with simple dark movable pupils
- a flexible half-lidded eye system for subtle acting
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation rendering

The character must remain immediately recognizable across poses, expressions, scenes, clothing, accessories and camera angles.

---

## 2. Character Role

The penguin is the quiet, restrained and subtly expressive member of the family.

Core personality:

- calm
- steady
- reserved
- quietly observant
- slightly socially awkward
- reliable
- emotionally subtle

The penguin should feel composed rather than sleepy, dull or emotionally flat.

---

## 3. Core Silhouette

The penguin has a compact pear-shaped body with a low center of gravity.

Required:

- rounded upper body
- slightly wider lower body
- short overall stature
- soft continuous silhouette
- short rounded flippers
- small flat feet
- no visible neck

Avoid:

- tall slim penguin anatomy
- long torso
- realistic bird anatomy
- long wings
- narrow waist
- sharp angular silhouette

---

## 4. Face / Cream Region

The warm-cream face and belly region is a major identity feature.

Required:

- simplified graphic treatment
- broad cream belly
- clean light face area
- soft continuous relationship between face and belly
- no realistic feather boundary complexity

The cream region may deform with perspective and acting.

Do not let it become:

- realistic penguin plumage
- highly detailed feather markings
- complex species-specific face patches
- broken into many small shapes

---

## 5. Eyes

The penguin uses a subtle high-control eye system.

Required:

- visible white eye areas
- simple dark movable pupils
- relatively close eye spacing
- readable gaze direction
- ability to use gentle half-lidding
- enough pupil travel for subtle side-eye

The half-lidded state is an acting capability and common neutral tendency, not a permanently sleepy face.

Allowed during acting:

- fully open eyes
- widening
- compression
- half-lidding
- full closure
- side-eye
- downward gaze
- slight asymmetry
- modest size changes

Avoid:

- tiny black-dot eyes
- permanently closed or sleepy eyes
- huge generic kawaii eyes
- anime irises
- glossy highlights
- eyelashes

The eyes should support subtle emotions such as awkwardness, calm, doubt, quiet amusement and restrained surprise.

---

## 6. Beak

The beak is:

- small
- simple
- warm orange
- rounded
- visually secondary to the eyes

It may open slightly during acting.

Avoid:

- long realistic beaks
- detailed nostrils
- sharp bird anatomy
- oversized beak shapes

---

## 7. Flippers

The flippers are:

- short
- rounded
- soft
- integrated into the body

They may:

- lift
- spread
- fold inward
- hug objects
- support broad gestures

Do not let them become:

- long arm-like limbs
- feathered realistic wings
- human hands
- sharp pointed wings

---

## 8. Feet

The feet are:

- small
- flat
- rounded
- warm orange
- visually stable and simple

They may lift, rotate or compress during acting.

Avoid realistic webbed-foot detail.

## 10. Rendering Language

Use the TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style.

Required:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- subtle natural imperfection
- controlled line-weight variation
- matte color blocks
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-like tonal variation
- finish quality matching the Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- raw sketch rendering
- heavy feather texture
- glossy AI mascot rendering
- perfect vector geometry
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- smooth synthetic gradients
- photorealism

---

## 11. Blush

Blush is NOT a permanent identity feature.

It may appear temporarily for:

- embarrassment
- shyness
- warmth
- excitement
- affection

Do not render blush in every neutral or ordinary scene.

## 14. Acting Rules

The penguin should act like a restrained but expressive animation performer.

Useful acting tendencies include:

- subtle side-eye
- downward gaze
- slight head/body tilt
- restrained flipper gestures
- half-lidded skepticism
- quiet awkwardness
- compact body compression
- sudden eye opening for surprise
- minimal but readable emotional shifts

Strong acting is allowed, but the penguin should not become hyperactive or generic-cute by default.
