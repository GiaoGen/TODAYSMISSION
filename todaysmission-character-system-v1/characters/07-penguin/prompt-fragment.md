# TODAYSMISSION Character Prompt Fragment
## Character 07 — Penguin

Version: 1.0
Status: LOCKED / PRODUCTION READY

---

## Character Identity

Use the canonical TODAYSMISSION Character 07 — Penguin.

Preserve the established identity exactly.

The character is:

- a compact pear-shaped deep-navy penguin
- warm-cream face and belly region
- short rounded flippers
- small warm-orange beak
- small warm-orange feet
- white expressive eyes
- simple dark movable pupils
- flexible half-lidded eye acting
- calm, restrained, quietly expressive emotional presence
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation style

The character must remain immediately recognizable as the same TODAYSMISSION penguin.

---

## Eye Identity

Preserve:

- visible white eye areas
- simple dark movable pupils
- relatively close spacing
- readable gaze
- half-lidded subtle acting capability

Eyes may fully open during surprise or strong emotion.

Do not replace them with:

- tiny black dots
- permanently sleepy eyes
- huge generic kawaii eyes
- anime eyes
- glossy eye rendering

---

## Face / Body Identity

Preserve:

- simplified warm-cream face region
- broad cream belly
- deep-navy rounded body
- compact pear-shaped silhouette
- low center of gravity
- short rounded flippers
- small orange beak and feet

Do not make the penguin:

- tall
- slim
- long-winged
- anatomically realistic
- detailed with realistic plumage

---

## Rendering Language

Use the established TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- subtle line-weight variation
- matte flat colors
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-style tonal variation
- finish quality matching the Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- rough sketch rendering
- realistic feather texture
- glossy AI mascot rendering
- perfect vector perfection
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- smooth synthetic gradients
- photorealism

---

## Acting Freedom

Preserve identity, not rigid geometry.

The character may freely change:

- pose
- expression
- pupil direction
- eye openness
- half-lid amount
- modest eye size
- beak opening
- flipper placement
- body tilt
- squash and stretch
- temporary blush
- camera angle

Subtle acting is especially encouraged.

---

## Emotional Direction

The penguin's core emotional quality is:

- calm
- steady
- reserved
- quietly observant
- subtly awkward
- reliable

Do not turn the penguin into a permanently sleepy, hyperactive or generic-cute character.

---

## Blush Rule

Blush is temporary emotional punctuation only.

It may appear for embarrassment, affection, warmth or excitement.

Do not make blush part of the neutral face.

---

## Accessories and Scene Freedom

Accessories, clothing and props may be introduced when appropriate.

They must never obscure or redesign the character's core identity.

The scene may freely determine:

- environment
- composition
- camera
- clothing
- accessories
- props
- lighting
- storytelling

---

## Hard Restrictions

Never:

- remove the deep navy body
- remove the cream face / belly
- make the character tall or slim
- replace the eyes with black dots
- remove the half-lidded acting capability
- make blush permanent
- add realistic plumage
- lengthen the flippers dramatically
- add a realistic long beak
- switch to glossy 3D mascot rendering
- redesign the character

---

## Production Principle

Character 07 is already designed.

Do NOT invent or reinterpret its appearance.

Spend creative freedom on:

ACTING
SCENE
STORYTELLING
COMPOSITION

while keeping the character identity stable.
