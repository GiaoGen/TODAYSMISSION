# TODAYSMISSION Character Prompt Fragment
## Character 03 — Owl

Version: 1.0
Status: LOCKED / PRODUCTION READY

---

## Character Identity

Use the canonical TODAYSMISSION Character 03 — Owl.

Preserve the established identity exactly.

The character is:

- a compact rounded ochre-brown owl
- large light cream face-disc / eye-mask region
- large expressive white eyes
- simple dark movable pupils
- small warm yellow-orange beak
- short rounded wings
- soft cream belly
- compact feet
- refined hand-drawn 2D animation style

The character must remain immediately recognizable as the same TODAYSMISSION owl.

---

## Eye / Face Identity

Preserve:

- one large unified light face-disc
- large white eyes inside the face-disc
- dark movable pupils
- highly readable gaze
- soft rounded geometry

Do not replace the eyes with tiny black dots.

Do not separate the eye system from the face-disc.

Avoid:

- anime eyes
- detailed irises
- glossy kawaii highlights
- eyelashes
- realistic eyes

---

## Body Identity

Preserve:

- compact round body
- short rounded wings
- small feet
- soft cream belly
- subtle ear-tuft silhouette
- low center of gravity

Do not make the owl:

- tall
- slim
- anatomically realistic
- long-winged
- predatory
- feather-detailed

---

## Rendering Language

Use the established TODAYSMISSION refined hand-drawn 2D animation style:

- clean hand-drawn contour
- subtle line-weight variation
- matte flat colors
- restrained dry-pencil texture
- very light cel-style tonal variation
- finish quality matching the Dinosaur Canonical Model

Avoid:

- rough sketch rendering
- detailed feather texture
- glossy AI mascot rendering
- perfect vector perfection
- 3D toy aesthetics
- plastic surfaces
- photorealism

---

## Acting Freedom

Preserve identity, not rigid geometry.

The character may freely change:

- pose
- expression
- pupil direction
- eye openness
- moderate eye size
- wing position
- body tilt
- beak opening
- squash and stretch
- camera angle

Strong asymmetry and gaze acting are encouraged.

---

## Emotional Direction

The owl's core emotional quality is:

- observant
- thoughtful
- curious
- insightful
- attentive

Its eyes and gaze should carry much of the acting.

---

## Accessories and Scene Freedom

Accessories, clothing and props may be introduced when appropriate.

They must never obscure or redesign the character's core identity.

The scene may freely determine:

- environment
- composition
- camera
- clothing
- props
- lighting
- storytelling

---

## Hard Restrictions

Never:

- remove the light face-disc
- replace the eyes with tiny black dots
- add realistic feather anatomy
- create long realistic wings
- add sharp talons
- make the character tall or slim
- switch to glossy 3D rendering
- redesign the character

---

## Production Principle

Character 03 is already designed.

Do NOT invent or reinterpret its appearance.

Spend creative freedom on:

ACTING
SCENE
STORYTELLING
COMPOSITION

while keeping the character identity stable.
