## Character Identity Summary

### 01 — Dinosaur

- Tiffany-blue body
- large warm-cream belly
- rounded deep-teal dorsal plates
- short thick tail
- large close-set white eyes
- no visible nose
- compact chubby low-center body
- strongest overall acting range

### 02 — Sheep

- warm-cream controlled cloud-like wool
- deep warm-gray face plate
- soft drooping ears
- medium white eyes with movable pupils
- compact rounded silhouette
- gentle / reassuring emotional role

### 03 — Owl

- ochre-brown compact body
- large unified light face-disc / eye-mask region
- large white expressive eyes
- small warm beak
- short rounded wings
- observant / thoughtful role

### 04 — Red Panda

- burnt-orange compact body
- visually large head
- restrained cream face markings
- small-to-medium white eyes
- tiny nose / mouth
- short dark paws
- oversized naturally attached striped tail
- warm / shy role

### 05 — Otter

- cocoa-brown short rounded body
- broad warm-cream belly / lower-face region
- very small round ears
- readable white eyes with blank gaze
- tiny simple nose / mouth
- thick soft tapered tail
- relaxed / slow-to-react role

### 06 — Rabbit

- warm-gray body
- warm-cream muzzle / belly
- long rounded ears with pink inner ears
- small-to-medium white eyes with quick directional gaze
- short thick limbs
- small round tail
- smart / quick-minded role

### 07 — Penguin

- deep navy compact pear-shaped body
- warm-cream face / belly
- short rounded flippers
- small orange beak / feet
- white eyes with movable pupils
- flexible half-lidded acting system
- calm / restrained role
