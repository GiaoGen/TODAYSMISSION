# TODAYSMISSION — Doing Things Alone

**Status:** Final Content Specification  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Pack Type:** Behavioural Challenge Pack  
**Public Missions:** 16  
**Hidden Final Mission:** 1

---

# 1. Pack Overview

## Pack Name

**Doing Things Alone**

## Pack Promise

> **Do the things you want to do — even when no one comes with you.**

## Core Problem

这个 Pack 面向的不是“不会独处”的用户，而是：

> 用户本来想做某件事情，却因为没有人陪、担心自己显得孤单、害怕别人注意到自己一个人，或单纯不习惯独自进入某些公共场景，因此取消、推迟或放弃行动。

典型想法包括：

- “没人陪就算了。”
- “一个人去会不会很奇怪？”
- “别人会不会觉得我没有朋友？”
- “这种地方大家都是一起去的。”
- “一个人待在那里不知道干嘛。”
- “以后有人陪我再去吧。”

## Core Avoidance Pattern

> **想做一件事 → 没人陪 → 推迟 / 放弃**

TODAYSMISSION 要改变的不是用户的性格，而是这个行为循环。

## Training Goal

通过覆盖不同公共程度、持续时间、社交属性和结伴预期的现实场景，让用户逐渐建立：

> **即使今天没有人陪，我依然可以去做自己想做的事情。**

这个 Pack 不训练：

- “享受孤独”
- “不需要任何人”
- “成为独行侠”
- “变成外向的人”

它训练的是：

> **行动不再依赖陪伴。**

# 2. Mission System

## 2.1 Free Pick

16 个公开 Mission **没有强制完成顺序**。

用户可以任意浏览、任意选择当前愿意面对的 Mission。

允许出现：

- 先做难的
- 先做简单的
- 跳着做
- 长期回避某几张

系统不要求：

> Mission 1 → Mission 2 → Mission 3

## 2.2 Difficulty Still Exists

“自由 Pick”不等于所有任务难度平级。

整个 Mission Pool 必须刻意包含：

- Low-friction missions
- Moderate discomfort
- High public visibility
- Strong companion expectation
- Longer independent activities
- Genuine personal challenge

不同用户对同一个 Mission 的主观难度可能完全不同，因此用户端不建议直接显示：

- Easy
- Medium
- Hard
- Level 1–5

后台可以维护大致强度数据，用于内容设计、审核与未来推荐。

## 2.3 Take This Mission

Mission 的核心循环：

> **Browse freely → Choose deliberately → Take → Lock → Complete → Choose again**

用户点击：

**Take this mission**

之后：

- 当前 Mission 被锁定
- 完成前不可切换到另一 Mission
- 完成后才能重新选择

因此每张 Mission 在 Take 前必须尽量让用户判断：

- 需要去哪里
- 是否需要特定设施
- 是否需要花钱
- 大约耗时
- 是否涉及他人
- 当前现实条件是否允许完成

## 2.4 Hidden Final Mission

16 个公开 Mission 全部完成后：

> **16 / 16 completed**

解锁第 17 张隐藏卡。

隐藏任务不是普通挑战，而是整个 Pack 的现实迁移：

# **STOP WAITING**

它要求用户面对自己现实生活中真正因为“没人陪”而一直搁置的事情。

# 3. Content Design Rules

所有公开 Mission 必须满足：

1. 与“缺少陪伴导致回避”直接相关。
2. 成功主要由用户自己的行为决定。
3. 不要求用户必须“不紧张”。
4. 不把“享受独处”作为完成条件。
5. 不为了挑战性故意制造危险、冒犯或社交骚扰。
6. 不为了数量重复同一个场景。
7. 整个 Pack 必须覆盖免费、低消费和消费型任务。
8. 整个 Pack 必须覆盖低设施依赖和高设施依赖任务。
9. 不允许所有任务都依赖电影院、咖啡馆、商场等城市设施。
10. Mission 可以具有现实门槛，但 Pack 整体不能只适用于大城市用户。
11. 任务必须有明确的 Learning Objective。
12. 一个 Mission 应主要训练一个核心心理变量。
13. 地点只是任务载体，不是任务定义本身。
14. 公开 Mission 负责训练；Final Mission 负责迁移到用户真实生活。

# 4. Public Mission Set

## Mission 01 — Stay Awhile

### Mission

**Spend 15 minutes alone in a public place.**

Pick a park, plaza, campus space, lobby, waterfront, public seating area, or anywhere people naturally spend time.

Try not to use your phone just to make yourself look busy.

### Learning Objective

训练用户体验：

> 我可以被别人看到一个人待着，而不需要马上离开，也不需要不断制造“我很忙”的感觉。

### Conditions

- Cost: Free
- Time: ~15 min
- Facility dependency: Very low
- Interaction required: No
- Public visibility: Low–Medium

---

## Mission 02 — Eat Outside Alone

### Mission

**Take something to eat or drink and have it alone in an open public place.**

A park bench, campus area, plaza, waterfront, public square, or another safe public space works.

Stay until you're finished.

### Learning Objective

训练：

> 我可以在别人存在的环境里，一个人正常停留并做自己的事情。

### Conditions

- Cost: Free–Low
- Time: 15–30 min
- Facility dependency: Very low
- Interaction required: No
- Public visibility: Medium

### Distinction

这张卡重点不是“一个人吃饭”，而是：

> **在开放公共空间中可见地独处。**

---

## Mission 03 — Lunch for One

### Mission

**Have a full meal somewhere by yourself.**

Use a cafeteria, food court, canteen, casual restaurant, or another place with public seating.

Eat there instead of taking it away.

### Learning Objective

训练：

> 别人可以明显看见我正在一个人吃饭，而这件事本身不需要解释。

### Conditions

- Cost: Low
- Time: 20–45 min
- Facility dependency: Low
- Interaction required: Minimal
- Public visibility: Medium

---

## Mission 04 — Browse Alone

### Mission

**Go somewhere you can browse at your own pace and stay for at least 30 minutes.**

A bookstore, library, market, shopping area, record store, hobby shop, or similar place works.

Don't rush just because you're there alone.

### Learning Objective

训练：

> 没有同行者时，我仍然可以按照自己的节奏观察、选择和停留。

### Conditions

- Cost: Free
- Time: 30+ min
- Facility dependency: Low–Medium
- Interaction required: No
- Public visibility: Low

---

## Mission 05 — Go Somewhere New

### Mission

**Visit a nearby public place you've never gone to alone before.**

It can be a neighborhood, park, waterfront, landmark, public garden, local attraction, or another safe place nearby.

Spend at least 30 minutes exploring it.

### Learning Objective

训练：

> 在没有熟悉同伴的情况下，我也可以进入一个不熟悉的环境。

### Conditions

- Cost: Free–Low
- Time: 30–60 min
- Facility dependency: Very low
- Interaction required: No
- Public visibility: Low–Medium

## Mission 06 — Sit in the Crowd

### Mission

**Spend 20 minutes alone somewhere busy.**

Choose a plaza, food hall, mall common area, campus center, station concourse, busy park, or another safe public space.

You're not waiting for anyone. You're just there.

### Learning Objective

直接挑战：

> “周围都是人，而我一个人在这里，会不会很奇怪？”

### Conditions

- Cost: Free
- Time: ~20 min
- Facility dependency: Low
- Interaction required: No
- Public visibility: High

---

## Mission 07 — Coffee for One

### Mission

**Go to a café alone and stay for at least 30 minutes.**

Order something, take a seat, and actually spend time there instead of leaving with it.

### Learning Objective

训练：

> 我可以独自占据一个具有明显社交属性的空间，而不需要一个同行者作为“理由”。

### Conditions

- Cost: Low
- Time: 30+ min
- Facility dependency: Medium
- Interaction required: Minimal
- Public visibility: Medium

---

## Mission 08 — Table for One

### Mission

**Have a sit-down meal at a restaurant by yourself.**

Ask for a table for one, order what you actually want, and stay through the meal.

### Learning Objective

训练更加明确的单人身份暴露：

> “我是一个人来这里吃饭的。”

用户完整经历：

- 入场
- Table for one
- 点餐
- 等待
- 用餐
- 离开

### Conditions

- Cost: Medium
- Time: 30–60+ min
- Facility dependency: Medium
- Interaction required: Minimal
- Public visibility: Medium–High

---

## Mission 09 — Movie for One

### Mission

**Go watch a movie by yourself.**

Pick a movie you actually want to see and stay through the whole thing.

### Learning Objective

挑战：

> “电影院这种地方需要有人陪，我才应该去。”

### Conditions

- Cost: Medium
- Time: 1.5–3 hr
- Facility dependency: High
- Interaction required: Minimal
- Public visibility: Medium
- Default companion expectation: High

### Role in Pack

这是 Doing Things Alone 的代表性 Mission 之一。

真正训练的不是“看电影”，而是：

> **不再因为没有同行者而取消自己想做的娱乐活动。**

## Mission 10 — See It for Yourself

### Mission

**Go somewhere people visit mainly for the experience — by yourself.**

Choose something available near you:

- museum
- gallery
- exhibition
- local attraction
- zoo
- aquarium
- historic site
- public garden
- cultural space
- another place people intentionally visit

Stay long enough to experience it at your own pace.

### Learning Objective

训练：

> 我不需要一个同行者，才能证明自己“有理由待在这里”。

同时体验：

> 我可以完全按照自己的兴趣和节奏行动。

### Conditions

- Cost: Free–Medium
- Time: 45–90 min
- Facility dependency: Medium–High
- Interaction required: No
- Public visibility: Medium

---

## Mission 11 — Play Alone

### Mission

**Do a recreational activity people often do with friends — by yourself.**

Choose something available near you:

- arcade
- bowling
- karaoke
- skating
- mini golf
- gaming café
- billiards
- batting cages
- another drop-in recreational activity

Complete at least one proper session or round.

### Learning Objective

训练：

> 即使某个娱乐活动通常和朋友一起做，我也可以因为自己想做而去。

### Conditions

- Cost: Low–Medium
- Time: 30–90 min
- Facility dependency: Medium–High
- Interaction required: Minimal
- Public visibility: High
- Default companion expectation: High

### Design Note

Mission 定义的是：

> **独自参加通常具有结伴属性的娱乐活动。**

而不是限定 Bowling、KTV 或某一种设施。

- another public event available near you

### Learning Objective

## Mission 13 — Show Up Alone

### Mission

**Attend a class, workshop, club session, or organized activity without bringing anyone with you.**

Stay for the actual session.

Don't just check the place out and leave.

### Learning Objective

训练：

> 我可以独自进入一个已经存在群体结构的环境。

### Conditions

- Cost: Free–Medium
- Time: 45+ min
- Facility dependency: Medium–High
- Interaction required: Possible
- Public visibility: High
- Default companion expectation: Medium

### Pack Boundary

如果任务要求：

> “主动认识三个人”

核心训练目标就会偏向 Talking to Strangers。

本 Mission 的成功条件只是：

> **一个人出现并参加。**

## Mission 14 — Be the Only One

### Mission

**Go somewhere alone where you expect most people to arrive with someone else.**

Choose a safe place or activity available to you.

It might be:

- a popular weekend spot
- an event
- an entertainment venue
- a local attraction
- a public celebration
- a performance
- a busy destination

Stay and do what you came there to do.

### Learning Objective

直接面对 Doing Things Alone 最核心的一类恐惧：

> “如果周围的人都有伴，只有我是一个人怎么办？”

训练目标不是寻找一个特定场所，而是：

> **在高结伴预期环境中保持自己的行动。**

### Conditions

- Cost: Variable
- Time: Variable
- Facility dependency: Variable
- Interaction required: No
- Public visibility: High
- Default companion expectation: Very high

## Mission 15 — One Hour Out

### Mission

**Plan one hour out by yourself — and don't make it just an errand.**

Choose at least two things to do while you're out.

For example:

> walk somewhere → browse somewhere → sit somewhere

The exact activities are up to you.

### Learning Objective

从单一场景推进到：

> 我可以自己组织一小段属于自己的公共生活。

### Conditions

- Cost: Free
- Time: 60+ min
- Facility dependency: Very low
- Interaction required: No
- Public visibility: Variable

---

## Mission 16 — Spend the Day Your Way

### Mission

**Plan half a day for yourself and spend it out alone.**

Choose at least two or three things you genuinely want to do.

Don't turn it into a checklist of errands.

Make it a real part of your day.

### Learning Objective

训练：

> 我不只是能独自完成某一个动作，我可以让一整段生活不依赖是否有人陪我。

这是公开 Mission 中最接近能力迁移的一张。

### Conditions

- Cost: Free–Variable
- Time: 3–6 hr
- Facility dependency: Variable
- Interaction required: Variable
- Public visibility: Variable

### Role in Pack

它连接：

> 单个独处场景

和最终的：

> **自己真正一直在等待别人陪的事情。**

# 5. Hidden Final Mission

# 🔒 STOP WAITING

## Unlock Condition

Only available after:

> **16 / 16 Public Missions Completed**

## Final Mission

> **What have you been waiting for someone else to do with you?**
>
> Something you actually want.
>
> Something you've kept putting off because no one would come with you.
>
> **Stop waiting. Go do it.**

## Examples

Examples are inspiration only.

The user may choose:

- Take a day trip to a city they've never explored.
- Go to a concert they've wanted to see.
- Visit a place saved for months.
- Go to a theme park.
- Take a class.
- Have the dinner.
- Go to the beach.
- Attend a festival.
- Visit a distant attraction.
- Make the trip.
- Do something completely different that matters personally to them.

## Final Learning Objective

前 16 个 Mission 都是由 TODAYSMISSION 提供的训练环境。

Final Mission 要求用户完成：

> **从产品规定的场景 → 迁移到自己的真实生活。**

最终改变的不是：

> “我做完了 16 个挑战。”

而应该是：

> **“我终于去做了那件一直因为没人陪而没有做的事情。”**

# 7. UI Ordering Principle

公开 Mission **不应该按照后台难度从低到高排列**。

不要让 UI 暗示：

> Mission 01 → Mission 16

是固定课程路线。

卡片应允许混合呈现：

- easy
- moderate
- uncomfortable
- challenging

例如：

```text
Coffee for One
Be the Only One
Browse Alone
Movie for One
Stay Awhile
Show Up Alone
...
```

目的：

> **Pick what you want.**

用户可以暂时回避高挑战任务。

但当进度来到：

> 13 / 16  
> 14 / 16  
> 15 / 16

那些长期被回避的 Mission 会自然成为剩余内容。

系统不需要强迫用户选择 Hard Mission。

> **未完成状态本身会逐渐暴露用户真正回避的场景。**

每个 Mission 建议至少保存以下内容属性：

```ts
primary_goal
public_visibility
default_companion_expectation
duration
cost_level
location_dependency
facility_dependency
interaction_required
social_exposure
estimated_intensity
```

这些字段主要服务于：

- 内容审核
- Mission Pool 平衡
- 未来推荐算法
- 个性化排序
- 防止内容重复

不需要全部展示给用户。

# 9. Mission Rejection Rules

以下类型原则上不进入 Doing Things Alone：

## Too Ordinary

普通人本来就经常独自完成、几乎不存在陪伴回避：

- Brush your teeth alone
- Grocery shopping alone
- Listen to music alone
- Sleep alone

## Too Private

没有公共暴露，也不涉及“因为没人陪而放弃行动”：

- Spend a day at home alone
- Watch a movie at home alone
- Cook alone

## Generic Self-Care Language

不采用模糊的：

- Take yourself on a date
- Learn to love yourself
- Enjoy your own company
- Practice self-love

Mission 必须描述实际可执行行为。

## Too Many Non-Core Variables

例如：

**Travel to another country alone**

可能同时受到：

- money
- passport / visa
- schedule
- safety
- transport
- lodging
- travel experience

影响。

这种任务可能非常有价值，但不适合作为所有用户都必须完成的标准公开 Mission。

它更适合作为：

- Final Mission inspiration
- 未来高阶 Pack
- Travel-specific content

---

# 10. Final Pack Philosophy

Doing Things Alone 不应该告诉用户：

> **Learn to love being alone.**

也不应该告诉用户：

> **You don't need anyone.**

真正的核心是：

> **No one is free. I can still go.**

前面的 Mission 是训练。

最后的 Mission 是现实生活。

最终目标不是让用户变成“更喜欢独处的人”，而是：

> **当用户真的想做一件事情时，“有没有人陪”不再拥有决定权。**
