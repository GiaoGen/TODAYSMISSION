# Doing Things Alone — Locked Pack Art Direction

## Pack Concept

Desired action should no longer depend on whether someone else comes with you. The collection translates self-directed public action, companion expectation, exposure, waiting, and sustained participation into hard-edged spatial abstraction without turning solitude into loneliness or self-care imagery.

## Abstraction Profile

- Semantic fidelity: medium–high
- Formal simplicity: medium–high
- Literal representation: low
- Human recognizability: none to fragmentary
- Spatial realism: distorted / flattened

## Formal Vocabulary

- Large hard-edged black, deep-blue, red, and warm-white planes
- Fragmented architectural and behavioral remnants
- Cropped object planes, bodily contours, thresholds, slots, bands, hinges, and voids
- Repetition only when it carries a social, temporal, or functional role
- Narrow cuts and restrained edge accents for timing, direction, or occupancy

## Spatial Grammar

Flat but depth-bearing construction with aggressive cropping, impossible joins, bold negative space, and strong scale/occupancy relationships. Viewpoint, hierarchy, direction, density, and focal placement must vary from work to work. Central corridors, frontal symmetry, and dominant warm-white polygons are exceptional—not defaults.

## Tension Mechanisms

Use a small rotating set: occupancy versus vacancy; containment versus entry; self-directed trajectory versus social rhythm; duration versus escape; scale pressure; interruption; directional conflict; and exposed stillness. “One versus many” is valid only when Mission-specific remnants make the relation precise.

## Color Behavior

Near-black is structural space. Deep blue carries depth, duration, movement, or an active behavioral plane. Red carries social pressure, thresholds, exposure, or decisive action. Warm-white marks attention, usable space, apertures, or semantic anchors. Roles may shift when the Mission requires it, but the palette remains restrained and matte.

## Complexity Budget

- Dominant formal systems: 1–2
- Mission-specific anchors: 1–3
- Repeated systems: maximum 1 principal rhythm
- Human fragments: only when behavior cannot be carried as clearly by space or objects
- Texture systems: 1 controlled matte pigment field
- Decorative marks without semantic purpose: prohibited

## Anti-Patterns

- Literal venue-and-protagonist scenes
- Lifestyle, wellness, cinematic, or inspirational-poster imagery
- Reusing an approved card's layout or foreground/background hierarchy
- Default central path, symmetrical framing, or giant warm-white subject plane
- Generic isolation metaphors or random geometry
- Decorative grain, glossy depth, and overfinished AI concept-art surfaces

## Rendering Mode

Raster generative art with hard-edged painted-plane construction and restrained physical pigment character.
