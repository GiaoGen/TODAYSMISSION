One Small Ask

Put Your Preference on the Table

During a real shared choice, state the option you genuinely prefer and ask whether the other person or group is willing to choose it.

Ask for Real Support

Ask a trusted person for one specific form of support you genuinely need, and allow them to say what they can or cannot offer.
