# Collection Planning

Plan the whole Pack before final generation. The goal is controlled variety,
not equal literal fidelity on every card.

## Semantic-distance Modes

- **Direct:** the Mission action or unmistakable outcome is present.
- **Associative:** connection comes through a prop, environment cue,
  relationship, aftermath, metaphor, visual rhythm, or transformed action.
- **Free:** character art prioritizes collection value and formal or emotional
  range while retaining a defensible Pack-level connection.

Use all modes intentionally. A 17-card Pack may begin near 7 direct, 7
associative, and 3 free, but this is a heuristic, never a quota. Reserve free
cards for deliberate collection needs. A card with no meaningful Pack
relation is irrelevant rather than free.

## Collection Matrix

Record at minimum for every Mission:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Mission | Stable title or ID |
| semantic mode | direct, associative, or free |
| connection | exact anchor retained |
| character | chosen protagonist or ensemble |
| acting beat | expression, gesture, and body intention |
| camera/crop | angle, distance, crop, and directional energy |
| composition | silhouette, balance, support shape, and negative space |
| action geometry | for dynamic scenes: origin, path, target, depth order, and vanishing direction |
| evidence | zero to a few indispensable cues |
| palette role | distinct collection role, not a background swap |
| confusion/safety | meanings and depictions to exclude |

## Three-Batch Production Plan

Assign every Mission Artwork to no more than three balanced production
batches before generation. Planning and Cover production are not Mission
batches. For 17 Missions, default to `6 / 6 / 5`; for another count, divide
the set as evenly as practical and use fewer than three batches when simpler.

Distribute semantic modes, cameras, scene scales, acting intensity, palette
roles, and technical risk across all batches. Batch 1 is formal production,
not a disposable style trial. An authorized repair remains inside its original
batch. Batch 3 includes the final collection-balance check and any still-unused
second calls needed to resolve a material collection issue. Never open Batch 4.

## Diversity Audit

Vary emotional register, camera, body logic, composition, scale, negative
space, palette structure, and semantic directness. Camera options include
overhead, side, low angle, rear view, close-up, wide, and extreme crop.

Do not solve diversity by changing only color or one prop. Nearby cards must
not share the same pose, camera, scene grammar, and emotional beat.

## Semantic Coverage Audit

The Pack as a whole must preserve its main training clusters. Literal coverage
may concentrate in selected cards; associative and free cards can expand
collectibility. Increase directness if the set becomes generic, and release
literalness if several Missions collapse into the same scene.
