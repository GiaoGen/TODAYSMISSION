# Approved Visual Baseline

Use the approved `Doing Things Alone` Red Panda collection as the primary
production-quality precedent for future Packs:

- Mission Artwork: `docs/design/packs/doing-things-alone/production-red-panda-v1/final-numbered-mission-artwork/`
- Pack Cover: `docs/design/packs/doing-things-alone/production-red-panda-v1/cover/pack-cover.png`

## What the baseline controls

- authored cinematic-storybook finish and collectible quality;
- tactile matte surfaces with species-appropriate material treatment;
- soft natural or motivated light, atmospheric depth, and gentle volume;
- coherent, believable environments that remain subordinate to the character;
- a protagonist that owns the image through hierarchy, action, light, color,
  or focus even when shown small in a wide scene;
- collection-scale variety in camera, crop, pose, scene scale, time of day,
  emotional register, and palette structure;
- clean integrated edges without heavy, uniform, sticker-like contour systems;
- a Cover built as one coherent Pack-level scene with useful overlay space,
  not a collage of Mission thumbnails.

## What the baseline does not control

Do not copy Red Panda identity, orange fur, furry material treatment, literal
locations, waterfront imagery, props, poses, cameras, or compositions into a
different Pack. Canonical character files still control identity, and the new
Pack handoff controls meaning and safety.

## How to use it

At collection planning, inspect the Mission set together and inspect the Cover
separately. For a generation call, attach only the canonical character images
and two to four baseline images that are relevant to the intended finish,
scene scale, light, or composition. Treat the selected baseline images as
quality and visual-language authority, never as literal content templates.
