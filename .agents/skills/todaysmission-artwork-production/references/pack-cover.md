# Pack Cover

Pack Cover is image-only background art. The frontend renders the title,
promise, Mission count, and all other text.

## Role

The Cover communicates Pack breadth and possibility, not one Mission. It may
be bolder and more editorial than Mission Artwork, with medium information
density. Prefer one coherent Pack-level world or event in which the protagonist
is actively oriented; an editorial object composition is also valid when it is
more expressive. Do not reduce the Cover to scattered icons, a checklist of
tokens, or a montage of tiny Mission scenes.

## Timing

Plan a provisional range early, but generate the final Cover last. Use the
finished Mission set to determine the actual acting, camera, palette, edge,
support-shape, and object vocabulary.

## Pack Evidence

Select only enough environmental, behavioral, or object evidence to suggest
the Pack's breadth without illustrating every Mission. Evidence may be embedded
naturally inside one complete scene instead of presented as separate tokens.
Every included element must communicate Pack possibility, locate the active
protagonist, or strengthen the composition.

## Cover Test

- communicates the Pack promise without text;
- suggests a range of possible experiences;
- preserves the protagonist as an active agent;
- avoids loneliness, rejection, danger, or another forbidden reading;
- differs from Mission art in breadth without becoming cluttered;
- matches the approved baseline Cover's scene coherence, production finish,
  atmospheric depth, and useful overlay space without copying its location,
  protagonist, props, or composition;
- leaves safe regions for frontend overlays without baking in typography.
